# NARRATIVE QA

## Core audit

### Plot logic
PASS with watchpoint. No coincidence is required for the core chain. The archive template, role reuse, 2012 event, and current digitization create the causal chain.

### Character motivation
PASS. An wants Khang. Khang wants proof. Vân wants to prevent incomplete records from becoming accusations. Each acts before the player and can resist player goals.

### Mystery fairness
PASS. Major reveals have multiple independent evidence paths. The player can infer “role” before the game confirms it.

### Twist fairness
PASS. Early counts, labels, photo inconsistencies, and role language foreshadow the multiple-occupant reveal.

### Timeline
PASS. 2012 → recurrence → 2026 digitization → Khang disappearance → player investigation is causal.

### Location logic
PASS. School night access is justified by the digitization/alumni archive context and loose after-hours routes. Outside-school locations exist because Khang rented a room while digitizing.

### Document logic
PASS with caution. The 09 template must look like a reused administrative form, not magical paperwork.

### Social logic
PASS. Vân's institutional response is motivated by reputational and evidentiary concerns rather than a caricatured evil authority.

### Theme
PASS. Theme appears through records, people, choices, and consequences.

### KCR
PASS. Eight KCR events are state-driven and each has a distinct sensory/spatial signature.

### Gameplay
PASS. Each chapter has an investigation identity; KCR changes physical observation rather than merely adding text.

### Pacing
PASS with watchpoint. Ch5–6 are the most vulnerable to repetition and should keep movement motivated by evidence dependency.

### Repetition
PASS. “find clue → reality changes” is not repeated mechanically; triggers and manifestations vary.

### Horror
PASS. No combat, no gore dependence, no constant jumpscare. The scare vocabulary is wrong counts, wrong positions, impossible audio, and institutional memory.

### Ending
PASS. Outcomes are concrete state combinations and pay off the central theme.

## Production red flags
1. Do not implement KCR with random procedural generation.
2. Do not spawn a generic ghost model as the Ninth.
3. Do not make Vân secretly orchestrate the supernatural mechanism.
4. Do not add hidden lore explaining KCR unless it strengthens the evidence chain.
5. Do not require the player to read every document.
